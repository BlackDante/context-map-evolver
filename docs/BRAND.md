## What the mark says

Three tiles, back to front: a **dashed outline** — the first whiteboard sketch of
a context map; a **tinted tile** — the same map, enriched; a **solid tile** — the
decision tool it evolves into. One artifact, gaining substance, never redrawn.
The white glyph on the front tile is the smallest possible context map: an
upstream context, a relation, a downstream context.

## Colours

| | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Violet 600 | `#7c3aed` | Primary. Front tile, UI accent, *core* subdomain. |
| Violet 300 | `#c4b5fd` | Middle tile. |
| Violet 400 | `#a78bfa` | Dashed outline. |
| Ink | `#0f172a` | Text on light. |
| Slate 500 | `#64748b` | Secondary text. |

Keep clear space of at least a quarter of the mark's width on every side, and do
not recolour, rotate, or separate the tiles.
